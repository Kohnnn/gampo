# Audio Sources

GamPo uses a mix of procedural audio generated in-repo and a small set of CC0
third-party casino one-shots.

## Procedural GamPo Audio

- Location: `public/audio/common`, `public/audio/cases`, `public/audio/slots`,
  `public/audio/bgm`, and route-specific generated loops under
  `public/audio/bgm/games`.
- Source: `scripts/genSfx.mjs` and `scripts/bgmEngine.mjs`.
- License/source: generated for this project, no external sample source.

## Kenney Casino Audio
